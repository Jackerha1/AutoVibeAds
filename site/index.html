AutoVibe

Video, web browsing and screen mirroring on your car's CarPlay screen — with ad blocking that stops video ads too.

Coming soon to the App Store

Features

Block ads

Banners, pop-ups and the ads before and during videos — gone, on every site. No setup needed. With AutoVibe Pro.

Your iPhone is the remote

Open a site or video on your iPhone and send it to the car. Play, pause, skip and seek from your phone — both screens stay on the same page.

Mirror your screen

Show any app on the car display. Start it from your iPhone, stop it from either screen.

Share to AutoVibe

In YouTube or any other app, tap Share, then AutoVibe. The video opens on the car screen.

Car controls

Play, pause and skip from the steering wheel, with the title and artwork on CarPlay and the lock screen.

Picture size

Auto makes every video as large as your screen allows, with nothing cut off. Or pick Fit, Fill or Stretch yourself.

A real browser on the car

Your favorite sites in their full desktop layout, sized for a wide car display.

Type or say it

A big car keyboard, or just dictate the address. Speech is recognized on your iPhone.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads in the app. Nothing you watch is collected.
