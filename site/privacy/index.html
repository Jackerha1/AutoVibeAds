Privacy Policy

AutoVibe doesn't collect your data.

Effective November 8, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. AutoVibe has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings — such as the picture size mode, your favorites and the sites where you turned ad blocking off — are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Websites you visit

AutoVibe includes a web browser. Sites you open receive the same information they would in any browser, and their own privacy policies apply to them.

Ad-block updates

AutoVibe periodically downloads updated ad-blocking filter lists from our server at autovibe.biz. These downloads contain no personal information and we keep no logs of them. The files are delivered through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address only to deliver them — see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Voice typing

If you use the microphone on the car keyboard, your speech is turned into text on your iPhone. AutoVibe doesn't keep or upload your voice. Microphone access is used only while you're speaking, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Screen mirroring and CarPlay

Mirrored video goes from your iPhone straight to your car's screen. It never leaves your devices and is never recorded or uploaded.

Feedback

If you tap Send Feedback or email us, we receive your email address, your message and the AutoVibe and iOS versions the app adds to it. We use them only to reply and to fix problems.

Purchases

AutoVibe Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or your Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active.

Children

AutoVibe is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
