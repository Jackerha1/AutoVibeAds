What do I need to see video on the car screen?

iOS 26.4 or later, and a car whose CarPlay system supports video. When your car can't show the picture, the video plays on your iPhone and you hear the sound through the car.

How do I play a video on my car?

Connect your iPhone to CarPlay and open AutoVibe on the car screen. In the Browser tab, pick a favorite or type an address — or play a video in AutoVibe on your iPhone and send it to the car. Video shows only while parked; while driving you hear the sound.

Can I control AutoVibe from my iPhone or the car screen?

Either one. Your iPhone is easiest to tap and type on: open a site or video and send it to the car, and your iPhone becomes the remote with play, pause, skip and a seek bar. On the car screen you can open favorites, type with the big car keyboard or your voice, and navigate. Both screens share one browser, so they always stay on the same page.

How do I mirror my whole screen?

On your iPhone, open the Mirror tab and start the broadcast. On the car screen, open AutoVibe's Mirror tab. To stop, tap Stop on the car screen or the red indicator at the top of your iPhone.

How do I send a video from another app?

In YouTube or any other app, tap Share, then AutoVibe. The video opens in AutoVibe and plays on the car screen.

Why doesn't the YouTube app's Cast button find my car?

Your car isn't a Cast device, so Cast can't find it. Share the video to AutoVibe instead, or use screen mirroring.

Why is there no picture in the car?

Your car decides when video can show — usually only while parked. Until then, you hear the sound.

Can I control playback from the car?

Yes. Play, pause and skip from the steering wheel, the CarPlay screen or the lock screen.

Why won't some streaming services play?

Paid services with copy protection only play in their own apps.

Part of the video is cut off, or has black bars.

AutoVibe fits each video to your screen automatically, and trims black bars that are part of the picture when it can. To choose yourself, tap the video on the car screen and pick Fit, Fill or Stretch, or set it in Settings under Aspect Ratio.

Why do websites look different on the car?

On the car screen, sites open in their full desktop layout, which fits a wide car display. On your iPhone they stay in their phone layout.

Can I type with my voice?

Yes. On the car keyboard, tap the microphone and say the address or search. Speech is recognized on your iPhone and isn't kept or uploaded.

Does AutoVibe block video ads?

Yes, with AutoVibe Pro. Ad blocking stops banners, pop-ups and the ads that play before and during videos on every site, with no setup.

A website looks broken.

With AutoVibe Pro, tap the shield in the address bar to turn ad blocking off for that site. The page reloads without blocking.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free plays web video and browsing on the car screen with no time limit, plus 30 minutes a day of screen mirroring, which resets at midnight. AutoVibe Pro adds unlimited screen mirroring, ad blocking that includes video ads, and video looping.

I have a code. How do I redeem it?

In AutoVibe, open the upgrade screen and tap Redeem Code. You can also redeem it in the App Store under your account.

Does it work with wireless CarPlay?

Yes. AutoVibe works with wired and wireless CarPlay.

How do I send feedback?

In AutoVibe, open Settings and tap Send Feedback, or email support@autovibe.biz. We read every message.
